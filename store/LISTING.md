## Description (paste into Store)
RoRev adds a compact earnings panel on Roblox experience pages so you can quickly see estimated total, monthly, and daily creator earnings — plus public stats like visits, favorites, and game passes.

How it works
• Reads public Roblox APIs for the experience you are viewing
• Estimates revenue with a transparent heuristic (Roblox hides real sales counts)
• Saves local snapshots on your device to improve daily/monthly trends over time
• Expand for more stats, or keep it collapsed for a clean view

Important
• Figures are ESTIMATES, not official Roblox analytics
• Not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or RoMonitor Stats
• Free and open source (MIT)
• No ads and no account required

Privacy
RoRev stores preferences and visit snapshots locally in your browser only. It does not run its own servers or sell your data.
