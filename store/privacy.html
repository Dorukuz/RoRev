Privacy Policy for RoRev

Last updated: August 6, 2026

RoRev (“the Extension”) shows estimated earnings and public stats on Roblox experience pages. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

What data we collect

RoRev does not collect personal accounts, names, emails, payment info, or analytics about you.

On your device only, the Extension may store:

  • Panel preferences (hidden / expanded)
  • Local visit snapshots for experiences you view (universe id, visit counts, timestamps) used to improve daily/monthly estimates

How data is stored

Preferences and snapshots are stored locally with chrome.storage.local on your browser. Nothing is uploaded to RoRev servers — RoRev has no backend.

Network requests

To show estimates, the Extension requests public experience data from Roblox APIs (for example games.roblox.com and apis.roblox.com) for the game page you are viewing. Those requests are made from your browser to Roblox. RoRev does not receive a copy of that traffic.

Third-party services

RoRev does not use analytics, ads, crash reporting, or other third-party tracking SDKs.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share user data. We do not operate a server that receives your browsing data.

Data retention and deletion

Local data stays until you clear it. You can remove it by uninstalling the Extension or clearing the Extension’s data in your browser settings.

Children’s privacy

RoRev is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the project repository or contact the publisher email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.

RoRev is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Estimates are heuristics only and are not official earnings data.
